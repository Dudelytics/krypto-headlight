### The Fed makes waiting more attractive

The Fed raised rates by **25 basis points to 3.75–4.00%** in September and paused in October. On Friday, Fed Governor **Lisa Cook** said September’s move was insufficient given persistent inflation. Markets expected another quarter-point increase in December. If short-term cash earns a little over four percent a year, you can afford to wait. Higher rates also reduce the present value of distant earnings at richly priced technology companies. That helps explain why so many investors cut risk at the same time on Friday. [Source 1](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/files/monetary20260916a1.pdf) · [Source 2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcminutes20261028.htm) · [Source 3](https://www.reuters.com/business/feds-cook-says-rate-hikes-needed-lower-high-inflation-2026-11-20/)

### Who actually pays the chip bill?

On Tuesday Nvidia reported **$115.8 billion in quarterly revenue**, up **103%** year on year. Two earlier agreements help explain why numbers like those can still make investors nervous.

In **November 2025**, Nvidia committed to invest up to **$10 billion** in Anthropic. Under the same set of deals, Anthropic committed to buy **$30 billion** of Microsoft Azure computing capacity powered by Nvidia chips. Part of what Nvidia puts into AI can therefore return via Microsoft as chip revenue. **CoreWeave** introduces a different risk. In a **September 2025** contract initially valued at **$6.3 billion**, Nvidia agreed under certain conditions to buy computing capacity through 2032 if CoreWeave could not find other customers for it. Nvidia can earn money on the chips today and pay for unsold capacity later. How much demand is supported by paying end customers, and how much are the companies underwriting for one another? [Source 1](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/) · [Source 2](https://www.anthropic.com/news/microsoft-nvidia-anthropic-announce-strategic-partnerships) · [Source 3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962825000047/crwv-20250909.htm)

That question took on added weight on Friday. Reuters reported that the **SEC’s enforcement division** was looking into possible accounting issues at Nvidia. A separate division handles routine filings reviews. It is unclear whether the financing deals are part of the inquiry. If significant revenue was booked too early or assumed risks were inadequately disclosed, investors would have to revisit their expectations for the AI boom. The stock was already down more than six percent before the report and fell further afterwards. [Source 1](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-secs-nvidia-probe-is-examining-potential-accounting-issues-sources-say-2026-11-20/) · [Source 2](https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/Article/filing-review-process---corp-fin.html)

### Two record IPOs and a place to park cash with interest

**SpaceX and Anthropic placed $205.2 billion of shares between them.** SpaceX reached **$85.7 billion** in June and Anthropic **$119.5 billion** in November. Anthropic’s total included **$93.5 billion** in the public offering with the over-allotment and **$26 billion** in a concurrent private placement. The buyers were **AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners**. The widely quoted **$81.3 billion** was the public offering before the over-allotment. [Source 1](https://www.reuters.com/business/anthropics-post-debut-stumble-delivers-reality-check-ai-trade-2026-11-20/) · [Source 2](https://www.anthropic.com/news/ipo-close)

**Our thesis is that some investors exited crypto before Anthropic’s IPO because they expected higher returns from AI stocks.** Anyone funding a large subscription has a reason to trim other positions over several days. The Anthropic offer was reportedly already **about six times oversubscribed** a week before the debut. And investors could earn interest while waiting for their shares. **Three-month US Treasuries yielded 4.34% annualised on 19 November.** Institutions held **$1.32 billion** more in US government money market funds the prior week. Compared with the **$4.61 trillion** in those funds, that was a small shift. The incentive was still there: keep cash available and earn interest while you wait. [Source 1](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/anthropics-ipo-is-about-six-times-oversubscribed-bloomberg-news-reports-2026-11-12/) · [Source 2](https://www.ici.org/research/stats/mmf) · [Source 3](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS3MO)

The tokenised Anthropic subscription through Binance Wallet gave us a direct view of money being set aside. Participants locked **USDC** in a smart contract and the campaign had a cap of **500 million USDC**. Binance later said it would distribute the limited allocation free of charge and return all USDC that had been locked. [Source 1](https://www.binance.com/de/support/announcement/detail/81e5668f2453429593002ad515a06b4c) · [Source 2](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/390009839769923)

**Friday brought the irony.** Imagine selling crypto because you expected bigger gains in AI stocks. On Anthropic’s second trading day you watch it come close to its offer price at one point, Nvidia lose around 14 percent and the Nasdaq fall more than five percent. The switch into AI put you in the very part of the equity market that got hit particularly hard on Friday. <h2 id="bitcoin">4. The Dude looks beyond productive crypto</h2>

### Bitcoin hits a two-year low and miners feel the squeeze

Bitcoin’s sell-off shows up in the arithmetic miners face every day. On Friday the same computing power earned miners an estimated **$28 in gross revenue per petahash per day**, about **26% less than a month earlier**. Electricity and operating costs still come out of that. JPMorgan’s model puts production costs at **$86,000 per bitcoin**. Miners with cheaper power or more efficient rigs can come in well below that. [Source 1](https://www.simplemining.io/hashprice) · [Source 2](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-20-jpmorgan-bitcoin-price-correction-gap-86000-production-cost-444763)

The estimated **seven-day average network hashrate was around 1,030 EH/s**. A lot of computing power was still online even as it earned fewer dollars per unit. If enough operators switch their machines off, mining difficulty may fall at a subsequent adjustment. That can give the miners who stay online some breathing room. [Source](https://www.simplemining.io/stats)

### GoMining lands in the Revolut app

GoMining says eligible Revolut customers in the **UK and European Economic Area** can now trade **GOMINING**. That puts the token in an app many people already use to manage their money. In the EEA it also carries regulatory weight. Revolut’s European crypto company is licensed under **MiCA**. Article 76 requires a trading platform to check a token’s technical reliability, links to possible fraud, and the issuer’s and development team’s experience and reputation, among other things, before admission. [Source 1](https://chainwire.org/2026/11/19/revolut-lists-gomining-token-for-15m-crypto-users/) · [Source 2](https://www.amf-france.org/en/warnings/white-lists/casp/revolut-digital-assets-europe-ltd) · [Source 3](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj)

GoMining users can use the token to pay ongoing mining fees and receive a discount. Wider distribution is especially valuable to GoMining if customers also use the token to pay. For miners, what still matters is how much of the Bitcoin reward remains after running costs. [Source](https://docs.gomining.com/en/product/gomining-token)

<h2 id="vertrauen">5. What trust means at Trezor, Binance and Sky</h2>

### Trezor — Who knows you own a hardware wallet?

Last week we looked at tampered **Safe 5 devices**. This week it is about the data generated when you buy a wallet. Trezor says data from roughly **27,000 orders** between 9 August and 7 November was probably copied from its fulfilment partner **Skladon**. It included names, delivery addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, wallet models and purchase prices. Trezor says recovery words, PINs and passphrases were outside the scope of the incident. [Source 1](https://headlight.dudelytics.com/en/editions/2026-11-14/) · [Source 2](https://trezor.io/blog/news/data-breach-at-trezor-shipping-partner-skladon)

When someone calls and knows your model and even the price you paid, it can easily sound like genuine support. That is what makes the data sensitive. Trezor already reports scam calls. If you get an unexpected message about your wallet, contact the manufacturer via its own website. Trezor now offers anonymous pick-up in **18 European countries** and plans to shorten order-data retention from **90 to 45 days**. [Source](https://trezor.io/blog/news/data-breach-at-trezor-shipping-partner-skladon)

### Binance — How do you serve a court order on a blockchain?

Following the record theft at Binance, an Abu Dhabi court ordered assets preserved. Binance puts the scope of the worldwide freezing order against the attackers at **$6.5 billion**. The lawyers handling the case say orders can be served onchain. A list of around **26,000 addresses** is to be updated continuously. Recovery ultimately depends on what can be located, frozen and actually returned as assets keep moving. [Source 1](https://www.reuters.com/business/binance-says-it-has-court-order-get-back-crypto-frozen-after-63-billion-heist-2026-11-18/) · [Source 2](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-binance-freezing-court-order-adgm-crypto-october-hack-443491)

### Sky — Nearly half of a lending pool is tied to one wallet

Imagine trying to withdraw a deposit when practically nothing is available because the money has been lent out. That is what happened in **stUSDS** this week. On 19 November **77.1 million USDS including interest**, or **48% of outstanding loans**, was tied to a wallet onchain analysts associate with Sky co-founder **Rune Christensen**. Bitquery says the wallet had made no principal repayments on the current lending platform since May 2025. The loans have no fixed maturity. [Source 1](https://bitquery.io/investigations/sky-stusds-rune-eth-loan) · [Source 2](https://sky.money/stusds)

Three of its four positions were below the protocol’s required collateral level on Sky’s own valuation. Normally the protocol could auction the pledged SKY tokens to cover the debt. Auctions were disabled for the entire loan type. In November 2025 Christensen himself proposed cutting the minimum collateral ratio from **145% to 120%** and capping the recognised SKY price at **2.5 US cents**. The proposal said this was to give borrowers more time to repay. The potential conflict is that he helped shape rules that also applied to loans attributed to him. The reporting does not establish who switched off auctions. [Source 1](https://forum.skyeco.com/t/stusds-and-sky-stake-borrow-liquidity-initiatives/27465) · [Source 2](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/rune-christensen-wallet-owes-half-sky-stusds-pool-debt)

The same wallet borrowed another **1 million USDS on 26 October**. Four days later, Danish video game studio **Raw Power Games**, founded by Christensen, said its financing had fallen through. It went into bankruptcy. It had reported a **DKK 87.0 million net loss in 2024** and **DKK 109.1 million in 2025**. The timing explains why people asked where the money went. The larger publicly traceable transfers end at **Bitcoin Suisse and Kraken**. No payment to the studio has been established. [Source 1](https://protos.com/wallet-tied-to-sky-founder-borrowed-1m-days-before-his-company-collapsed/) · [Source 2](https://bitquery.io/investigations/sky-stusds-rune-eth-loan) · [Source 3](https://cvrbase.dk/nyheder/raw-power-games-aps-er-taget-under-konkursbehandling-41456841)

Things looked different for the **USDS** stablecoin. After an investigation into transactions allegedly connected to illicit activity, users redeemed **more than $2 billion** worth of USDS. Sky disputes key conclusions of the investigation. USDS held its dollar peg. In the separate **stUSDS lending pool**, depositors had to wait for new deposits or repayments when utilisation was full. One question was whether the dollar price would hold. The other was whether you could withdraw your money at that moment. [Source 1](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/11/19/sky-s-usds-stablecoin-shrinks-35-as-usd1-5-billion-exits-reserves-after-illicit-finance-report) · [Source 2](https://cointelegraph.com/news/sky-protocol-sees-2b-in-stablecoin-withdrawals-disputes-illicit-fund-claims) · [Source 3](https://sky.money/stusds)

<h2 id="europa">6. Europe’s digital markets need to work together</h2>

If you invest in a fund, you want to know who is looking after the assets. On 19 November Germany’s **BaFin** issued Circular **15/2026 (WA)** clarifying what that means for cryptoassets and DLT-based financial instruments. Under the circular, managers may not themselves custody cryptoassets held for their funds. Among other requirements, the depositary must be able to establish which fund the holdings belong to. [Source](https://www.bafin.de/SharedDocs/Veroeffentlichungen/DE/Rundschreiben/2026/rs_15_2026_kryptowerte.html)

The next day Christine Lagarde took the question up a level. What does Europe gain from new venues for tokenised securities if they cannot settle trades with one another? The ECB is working on shared standards. Common rules are up to lawmakers. For a sense of the distance from infrastructure to adoption, take **Wero**. Handelsblatt reported that the European payment service was available at none of Germany’s ten biggest online stores by sales. The checkout tells you fairly quickly how far a payment system has come.
