# Privacy and readership statistics

Updated: 14 November 2026. This supplement applies to Crypto-Headlight at headlight.dudelytics.com. The controller is Axel Lämmle, Sternstraße 10, 82256 Fürstenfeldbruck, Germany; contact: webmaster@dudelytics.com. The [general privacy notice](https://dudelytics.com/datenschutz/) provides further information on your rights and contact options. This page is the English translation of the corresponding German Headlight notice.

## Your choice

Readership statistics are enabled by default and start automatically when JavaScript is available and no opt-out or privacy signal prevents measurement. You can disable them under “Manage readership statistics” in the footer. We store only your opt-out decision in local browser storage, without an individual identifier. It applies to this website in this browser, across both language editions and after reloading, until you enable measurement again or clear browser storage. If browser storage is blocked, disabling measurement applies only to the current page view. Data already transmitted is not deleted retrospectively. We respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Reading is possible independently of measurement.

## Purpose and measurements

We want to understand which editions attract interest. We measure page views, the public page path and hostname, the referring domain without its path or query parameters and, for articles, one-time thresholds for active time (30, 120 and 300 seconds), reading depth (25, 50, 75 and 90%) and clicks on sources or discussion links. Active time is counted only while the window is visible, focused and has had recent interaction. These figures indicate usage; they do not prove that an article has actually been read.

Our measurement code does not transmit form entries, email addresses, account details, URL query parameters, fragments or full destinations of clicked links. We do not assign our own visitor identifiers or use analytics cookies or local-storage identifiers. The local opt-out flag contains only “1”. We do not record sessions or heatmaps.

## Provider and technical processing

The recipient is Umami Software, Inc., USA. Even without cookies, establishing a connection involves an IP address and browser information. According to the provider, IP addresses are temporarily processed for delivery, calculation of visitor identifiers and approximate geolocation; technical security logs may contain them. Visitor counts are therefore estimates, not counts of uniquely identified people.

Umami Cloud offers US and EU storage regions. Exclusively European processing is not guaranteed here: delivery, security operations and support may involve other countries. Contractual provisions on data processing, subprocessors and international transfers are available in the [Umami DPA](https://umami.is/dpa) and [subprocessor list](https://umami.is/subprocessors). Further information: [Umami privacy policy](https://umami.is/privacy).

Dudelytics bases this statistical analysis on its legitimate interest in improving its public information offering (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object to further collection using the disable button.

## Retention and analysis

Under the intended free Hobby plan, the provider states that analytics data is retained for six months. Technical logs and backups are subject to the provider's separate retention periods. The analytics dashboard is not publicly shared. Dudelytics does not link this information to personal user accounts or advertising profiles.

Statistics cover technically reachable page views where measurement has not been disabled. Blockers, privacy signals, opt-outs and our own test visits affect the figures. We do not extrapolate total visitor numbers from them.

## Hosting

Headlight pages are served through GitHub Pages. GitHub processes necessary connection data independently of Umami measurement. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). References to STRATO in the general Dudelytics notice describe the main website hosted there.

## Crypto-Headlight by email

The free email subscription is optional and independent of reading the website. We use Brevo for signup, confirmation, delivery and unsubscribe handling. The signup form asks only for your email address and consent. This is personal data; statements about data-minimising readership statistics do not replace these separate notices. The English edition has its own signup form, English confirmation email and recipient list. The German newsletter has a separate list. Signing up for either language does not automatically subscribe you to the other.

After signing up, you receive a confirmation email. Your address is added to the Headlight mailing list only after you select the confirmation link. We use it to send new editions. You can withdraw your consent for the future at any time using the unsubscribe link in each edition or by contacting webmaster@dudelytics.com.

Brevo also processes signup and confirmation status, plus technical connection and delivery information such as timestamps, IP addresses and delivery errors. These support operation, delivery, abuse prevention and proof of signup. Your address is used for delivery during the subscription. After unsubscribing, it is blocked from further newsletters; a suppression record and necessary evidence may be retained longer to ensure the unsubscribe is respected. Unsubscribing therefore does not automatically erase every technical log. Requests for deletion or access can be sent to the contact address above.

See [Brevo's privacy policy](https://www.brevo.com/en/legal/privacypolicy/) for information on the delivery provider's processing. The form opens on a Brevo domain; subscriber data is not stored in the public GitHub repository.
