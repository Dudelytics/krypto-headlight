# About Crypto-Headlight

Dudelytics Crypto-Headlight is a public, free weekly report on the productive crypto market. It is published by Dudelytics / Axel Lämmle.

We examine the main DPMI, its sectors, sentiment and market stress using available data. Each report specifies its comparison period, data timestamps, methodology and sources. News, established observations and hypotheses are kept distinct.

## Data and limits

Source time and archive capture time are different. Missing or stale data is identified. The [public values archive](https://github.com/Dudelytics/dpmi-public-data) is not a substitute for an independent audit and does not provide immutable proof of publication.

CoinGecko is a raw data provider, not the publisher or a sponsor. The [methodology](https://dudelytics.com/dpmi/methodik/) and [usage terms](https://dudelytics.com/lizenzen/) continue to apply. No investment advice.

The DVIX combines measured market fluctuations and stress signals. Its points are neither percentage losses nor a probability of a crash. The thresholds are model categories; they do not predict future prices. Basis: [public DVIX methodology, version 0.2](https://dudelytics.com/data/dpmi/dvix.php?view=methodology).

### Fear & Greed: sentiment in the productive crypto market

This indicator also runs from 0 to 100: low values indicate fear, high values greed. It combines the short-term 24-hour pulse with the seven-day trend and the 30-day regime. The classifications in Headlight come from the [Dudelytics dataset](https://dudelytics.com/data/dpmi/fear-greed.php), including its separate labels for the three horizons. This is not the similarly named whole-market indicator offered by other providers.

The exact category thresholds are not specified in the dataset currently linked. We therefore do not publish an unverified threshold table. A neutral reading is neither a buy recommendation nor a claim that the market is risk-free.

### What the abbreviations mean

- **DPMI:** Dudelytics Productive Market Index; our index for the productive crypto market selected under the [DPMI methodology](https://dudelytics.com/dpmi/methodik/).
- **Holder Quality / HQ:** A selection of 30 assets in the documented “evidenced” class. The name describes a selection rule, not a guarantee of returns or smaller losses. Weighting follows market capitalisation, capped at 15% per asset, without an additional quality factor.
- **SF:** Sector family — indices for individual areas such as DeFi or RWA. **BENCH** denotes the market comparison and **FG** Fear & Greed. The appended numbers identify the methodology version.
- **TEMPORAL-WARMUP:** A technical version suffix for the gradual construction of longer sentiment horizons. It does not automatically mean the current dataset is incomplete: its reported time maturity and data coverage are what matter.
- **Hourly anchors:** Reference observations on an hourly grid used to calculate changes over time. Coverage indicates how complete that series is; missing observations are not invented in Headlight.
- **Net flow:** Inflows minus outflows over the stated period. A transfer from an exchange to your own wallet is not a sale and cannot simply be added to ETF outflows.
